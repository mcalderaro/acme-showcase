## Why operations deserve their own section

A lot of teams talk about shipping. Fewer teams make the mechanics of shipping easy to inspect.

ACME treats operations as part of the story, not background noise. The routing, handoffs, review gates, and documentation steps are what make the company understandable from the outside.

That matters because the public site is not just showing what ACME built. It is showing how ACME stays coherent while building with an AI-assisted team.
