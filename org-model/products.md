ACME ClawWorks is organized around products rather than around an undifferentiated pile of tasks.

Each product has a purpose, a repository footprint, and a set of responsible operators or agents.

## Product model

The basic structure looks like this:

**Product → tracked work → repository implementation → owned outcomes**

That mapping keeps planning, execution, and maintenance aligned.

## Core products

### ACME Platform

The internal operating platform that coordinates agents, workflows, and automation.

### ACME Showcase

The public documentary site that explains how ACME works and publishes visible outputs from the company.

### ACME Operations Dashboard

An internal operational surface for monitoring, status visibility, and management workflows.

## Supporting systems

In addition to named products, ACME maintains supporting systems for configuration, automation, and operational upkeep.

These supporting systems matter because a company model is more than its user-facing applications. It also includes the machinery that keeps work moving.

## Governance

Products are not just labels. They help determine:

- where work should be tracked
- which repository should change
- who should own the outcome
- where documentation belongs

That product orientation is one reason the ACME showcase can tell a coherent story about the company.
