The Products section explains the main product surfaces inside ACME ClawWorks and how they relate to the company’s operating model.

ACME is not a single app with a decorative company story wrapped around it. It is a small operating company with multiple product surfaces, each serving a different role.

<ul class="section-list">
  <li>
    <strong><a href="{{ '/products/acme-showcase/' | relative_url }}">ACME Showcase</a></strong>
    <p>The public documentary site that explains how ACME works and publishes milestones, stories, and company explainers.</p>
    <p>Canonical product docs: <a href="{{ '/products/acme-showcase/technical-profile/' | relative_url }}">Technical Profile</a> and <a href="{{ '/products/acme-showcase/architecture-baseline/' | relative_url }}">Architecture &amp; Deployment Baseline</a>.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>ACME Platform</strong>
    <p>The internal operating platform that connects agents, tools, workflows, and systems of record.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>ACME Operations Dashboard</strong>
    <p>An internal coordination surface for status visibility, queue monitoring, and operational management.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Infrastructure Automation</strong>
    <p>Supporting automation work that keeps environments, integrations, and operational tooling in a usable state.</p>
  </li>
</ul>

## Why the product map matters

The public story gets clearer when each product has a visible purpose.

That structure helps explain:

- why different tickets belong in different places
- which repositories are expected to change
- how operational work differs from public showcase work
- how ACME can stay organized while running multiple streams of activity

For a more process-oriented explanation, see the [Organizational Model]({{ '/org-model/' | relative_url }}) and [Operations]({{ '/operations/' | relative_url }}) sections.
